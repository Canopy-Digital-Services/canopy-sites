---
title: Editing with other people
description: Who can edit your site, why only one person edits at a time, and what happens to changes someone left unpublished.
icon: Users
---

More than one person can sign in to your Canopy Sites account, each with their own email and password. Everyone on the account can edit the site, preview changes, and publish them.

## Adding or removing someone

Canopy adds people to your account. Send Michael the person's name and email address, and they'll get a link to set their own password. The link works once and lasts a week.

To take someone off the account, ask the same way. They're signed out and can no longer sign in. What they published stays on the site.

## One person edits at a time

The site becomes yours to edit when you start a change: opening a piece of text in the **Editor**, or sending a message in **Quick Edit**. Having a page open doesn't hold the site.

While you're editing, everyone else sees who has the site, for example "Jane Doe is currently making edits". It shows in place of the Quick Edit chat, and over the site in the Editor. They can still open **Preview** to see what you've changed, and Forms and Stats work as usual.

The site is free for the next person when you:

- **Publish** or **Discard** your changes
- leave Quick Edit, the Editor, and Preview for about a minute and a half
- go ten minutes without typing or clicking on those pages

The next person's page opens up by itself within half a minute. There's nothing to reload.

## Unpublished changes belong to whoever made them

If someone made changes and didn't publish them, nobody else can edit until those changes are published or discarded. You'll see whose they are, for example "Jane Doe has unpublished changes", with a list of what she changed.

From there you can:

- Message her and ask.
- Click **Preview**, look the changes over, and **Publish** them.
- Click **Discard** to remove them. It asks first, and names whose changes they are.

After that the site is yours to edit. This keeps one person's unfinished changes from going live along with someone else's.

Text typed into a field but not confirmed with **Done**, Enter, or a click on another field isn't part of anyone's changes. It can't be published until it's confirmed.

## If your text wasn't saved

If someone else changes the site while you have a piece of text open, the Editor loads the site again with their change. A message shows what you had typed, with a **Copy** button, so you can put it back.

## Who published what

On Quick Edit, **Recently published** lists each change with the person who published it.
